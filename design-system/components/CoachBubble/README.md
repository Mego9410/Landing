# CoachBubble

A chat bubble in the Coach tab: the coach speaks in `lilac`, the person in raised white.

- Provide: `children`, optional `from` (`coach` default, or `you`) and `redirect`.
- `redirect` turns a coach bubble `sky` with a "For your prescriber" tag. Use it for every medication, dose, taper or stopping question, with this wording: "That's a decision for your prescriber, so I can't help with doses. I can make a one-page summary of your trend and habits to take with you."
- Coach replies: short, warm, practical, `body` size, no emoji, no calorie numbers below the safe floor.
