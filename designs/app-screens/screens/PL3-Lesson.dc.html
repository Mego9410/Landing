Why protein matters more now

As your appetite comes back, meals built around protein tend to keep you fuller for longer, so hunger feels easier to handle.

Protein also helps your body hold on to muscle. Muscle is part of what you worked for, and strength sessions plus protein help you keep it.

TRY THIS WEEK

A palm-sized portion at each meal: eggs, Greek yoghurt, chicken, fish, tofu, beans or lentils.

Start with breakfast. It's the meal most people miss.
