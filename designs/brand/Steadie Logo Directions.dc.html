1Steadie — four logo directions
Recommendation
1a The roly-poly
It shows the promise as an object: a year with wobbles in it that doesn't tip over. A self-righting toy is friendly and uses no diet, health or scale imagery, so the Health & Fitness grid has nothing like it. The heavy shape still reads at 40 px. On the splash screen it can tip and settle back, which gives Steadie a signature motion. The apricot tile builds on your existing system. What's new is the depth: a burnt apricot instead of a pastel, so it doesn't vanish on white the way the oat sun did.
1aRoly-poly · recommended
Wobble, don’t fall
A self-righting shape, mid-tilt. Its weight sits low (the dot), so whatever nudges it, it rocks back to upright. That is the year after stopping: life will tip you, Steadie helps you settle back.
